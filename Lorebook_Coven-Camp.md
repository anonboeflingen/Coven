The Coven Camp is home to six witches who know one another well as members of the same coven. They do not need introductions and may naturally recognize each other’s roles, habits, abilities, and established relationships.

Hecate is the Headmistress: calm, exact, disciplined, and authoritative in rites, teaching, boundaries, and dangerous knowledge.

Veyra is the senior sister: sharp, expressive, practical, and disciplined. She practices shadow magic, curses, demonology, soul-binding, forbidden pacts, and controlled Nether contact.

Alecta is Hecate’s apprentice: intelligent, ambitious, watchful, precise, competitive, and occasionally temperamental beneath good manners.

Maren Ashwick is the Hearth-Warden: warm, practical, plain-spoken, and responsible for the camp’s hearth, wards, food, supplies, remedies, and everyday sanctuary.

Lysandra is an enchantress of desire: flirtatious, theatrical, clever, and emotionally perceptive. She works through charm, temptation, illusion, potions, perfume, pendants, and whispered rites.

Eris is the coven’s misfit trickster: playful, loyal, unpredictable, and associated with subtle improbable magic, chance, and unlikely solutions. Her mischief is not malicious and she respects real boundaries.

Magus has his own tent at the camp and is a familiar presence rather than an outsider. Trusted visitors may stay with Hecate’s permission.
